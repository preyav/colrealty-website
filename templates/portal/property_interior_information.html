General Field Help:

A list of features or description of the interior of the property included in the sale/lease.

If the primary bedroom is on the main level, select Primary Bedroom on Main under Interior Features. Where room-level details are maintained, record the corresponding room type and level there as well.

  • 2 Primary Baths: The property has two primary bathrooms.
  • 2 Primary Suites: Two primary bedrooms/suites to accommodate extended family.
  • Bar: A built-in or movable fixture for storing, preparing, serving, or consuming drinks.
  • Bidet: A fixture designed for washing the lower body.
  • Bookcases: Shelves for books or other objects that may or may not be built in.
  • Breakfast Bar: An eating surface, typically smaller than a dining table and attached to other kitchen surfaces.
  • Built-in Features: Features physically attached to the structure.
  • Cedar Closet(s): A closet partially or fully lined with cedar wood.
  • Ceiling Fan(s): The rooms have ceiling-mounted fans.
  • Ceiling(s)-Beamed: The rooms have exposed beams across the ceiling.
  • Ceiling(s)-Cathedral: A type of vaulted ceiling, typically higher than normal, with a slant or curve reaching an uppermost point at an equal distance from two shorter walls.
  • Ceiling(s)-Coffered: A ceiling with multiple decorative indentations, trays, or sunken panels.
  • Ceiling(s)-High: The ceiling height is greater than a normal ceiling height.
  • High Speed Internet: The property has access to high-speed internet service but may not be wired or connected to it.
  • Ceiling(s)-Tray: An inverted tray or recessed area, often rectangular, that adds depth and interest to a ceiling.
  • Ceiling(s)-Vaulted: Typically a high ceiling with no attic between the ceiling and roof. A vaulted ceiling whose two angles meet in the center may be called a cathedral ceiling.
  • Central Vacuum: A built-in vacuum with a power/collection unit, typically in a garage or closet, tubing to rooms, and wall-mounted receptacles for a movable vacuum hose.
  • Chandelier: A decorative lighting fixture with several lights or candles and decorative components such as glass or crystal.
  • Crown Molding: Decorative trim covering the seam between the ceiling and walls.
  • Double Vanity: Bathroom cabinetry with two built-in sinks.
  • Dry Bar: A built-in or movable fixture for storing, preparing, serving, and consuming drinks without a water supply or sink.
  • Dumbwaiter: A small elevator, typically used to carry food between floors.
  • Eat-in Kitchen: A kitchen designed to accommodate dining.
  • Dryer-Electric Hookup: The property has a hookup for an electric dryer.
  • Dryer-Gas Hookup: The property has a hookup for a gas dryer.
  • Elevator: A platform or compartment in a shaft used to raise or lower people or objects.
  • Entrance Foyer: A room or hall at the entrance leading to other parts of the structure.
  • French Doors: Usually a pair of lightweight doors with glass panes throughout their length.
  • In-Law Floorplan: An area with the characteristics of an independent apartment, typically including a living area, kitchen, bedroom, and bathroom. It is not necessarily an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU).
  • Intercom: An electrical device for one-way or two-way communication, such as a public-address or paging system.
  • Interior Steps: Any number of steps, up or down, inside the dwelling.
  • Kitchen Island: A separate kitchen counter surface, unattached to walls or other surfaces. It may include a sink, stove, or other fixtures.
  • Low Flow Plumbing Fixtures: Some or all plumbing fixtures are designed to save water.
  • Natural Woodwork: The property or room has features made from real wood.
  • No Interior Steps: No existing stairs or steps inside the dwelling.
  • Open Floorplan: A floor plan using large open spaces and avoiding small enclosed spaces.
  • Pantry: A small room or closet for storing food, dishes, and utensils.
  • Primary Bedroom on Main: The primary bedroom is on the main level of the structure.
  • Recessed Lighting: A light fixture installed into a hollow opening in the ceiling.
  • Sauna: A small room or separate structure designed to produce wet steam heat or dry heat to induce perspiration.
  • Smart Home: Electronic automation of lighting, heating/cooling, security, and other amenities, typically connected through an app or software and third-party services, offering convenience and energy efficiency.
  • Smart Thermostat: A heating/cooling control with convenience and energy-saving features. It may integrate with a larger smart-home system and typically uses a third-party service.
  • Soaking Tub: A bathtub typically deeper, and possibly shorter, than a traditional tub.
  • Solar Tube(s): A reflective tube extending from a light-gathering surface on the roof into a room, where outside light is distributed.
  • Sound System: A sound system typically including in-wall wiring, recessed or built-in speakers, and a built-in location for an amplifier or other audio equipment.
  • Stackable W/D Connections: Connections for a stacked clothes washer and dryer.
  • Storage: The property or room has storage space.
  • Study/Home Office: A study or home office. A bedroom used, or usable, as a home office does not count as a Study/Home Office. A floor plan showing the study/home office is recommended to verify data entry, although not required to select this feature.
  • Tile Counters: Counters made from tile.
  • Track Lighting: Light fixtures mounted on a track that allows their positions to be adjusted.
  • Two Primary Closets: The room has two separate closets.
  • Walk-In Closet(s): A closet that is a small room with an entryway.
  • WaterSense Fixture(s): Water fixtures backed by independent third-party certification that meet EPA specifications for water efficiency and performance.
  • Wet Bar: Commonly a built-in fixture for storing, preparing, serving, or consuming drinks, with a faucet and sink.
  • Wired for Data: The property is wired for data, typically with Category 5 or 6 wiring for Ethernet communications.
  • Wired for Sound: Wiring for a built-in sound system, typically including in-wall wiring, recessed or built-in speakers, and an audio equipment location. Equipment may not be included.
