General Field Help:

A list of features or description of the exterior of the property included in the sale/lease.

  • Balcony: The property has an exterior balcony.
  • Barbecue: The property has an outdoor barbecue.
  • Basketball Court: The property has a basketball court.
  • Boat Dock - Private: A dock attached to the property designated only for the owner of the land.
  • Boat Dock - Shared: The property includes a dock.
  • Boat Lift: The property has a boat lift, ship lift, or lift lock, a machine for transporting boats between water at two different elevations, as an alternative to a canal lock or canal inclined plane.
  • Boat Lock: The property has a boat lock, infrastructure that lifts a boat from a lower body of water to a higher body of water without removing it from the water.
  • Boat Ramp: A ramp on the shore by which ships or boats can be moved to and from the water, also called a slipway.
  • Boat Slip: The property includes a boat slip.
  • Courtyard-Covered: The property has a covered courtyard.
  • Courtyard-Uncovered: The property has an uncovered courtyard.
  • Dog Run: A dedicated area, usually enclosed by a fence, where dogs may exercise, play, or lie down.
  • Electric Car Plug-In: A station to recharge cars that run on electricity or solar power, such as hybrid vehicles.
  • Electric Grill: The property has an outdoor electric grill.
  • Exterior Steps: Any number of steps, up or down, on the outside of the dwelling.
  • Garden: The property has a garden.
  • Gas Grill: The property has an outdoor gas grill.
  • Gray Water System: The property has a gray water system.
  • Gutters-Full: Rain gutters installed along all roof edges divert rain to downspouts that direct water away from the foundation.
  • Gutters-Partial: Rain gutters installed along some roof edges, typically the front or over doors, divert runoff to downspouts.
  • Kennel: The property has a kennel.
  • Lighting: The property has exterior lighting.
  • Misting System: A fixture that sprays water mist for dust control or to help cool a patio/porch and its occupants.
  • No Exterior Steps: No existing stairs or steps outside or around the home/dwelling.
  • Outdoor Grill: The property has an outdoor grill.
  • Permeable Paving: Paving that allows fluids to pass through to the ground below or to channeling.
  • Pest Tubes in Walls: Small plastic tubes installed in walls during construction, periodically injected with insecticide, usually through an annual contract, to treat or prevent pest activity.
  • Playground: The property has a playground.
  • Private Entrance: The property has a private entrance.
  • Private Yard: The property has a private yard.
  • Restricted Access: The property has restricted access.
  • RV Hookup: The property has hookups for recreational vehicles.
  • Satellite Dish Leased: The property comes with a leased satellite dish.
  • Satellite Dish Owned: The property comes with an owned satellite dish.
  • Sport Court: The property has shared sport courts or facilities usable for various sports, such as pickleball.
  • Tennis Court(s): The property has one or more tennis courts.
