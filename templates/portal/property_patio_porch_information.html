General Field Help:

A list of features or description of the patio or porch included in the sale/lease.

  • Arbor: A shelter of vines, branches, or latticework covered with climbing shrubs or vines on the patio/porch.
  • Awning(s): The property has awnings.
  • Covered: The property has a covered patio or porch.
  • Deck: The property has a deck.
  • Enclosed: The property has an enclosed patio or porch.
  • Front Porch: The property has a front porch.
  • Glass Enclosed: The property has a glass-enclosed patio or porch.
  • Mosquito System: A mosquito-repellent application system that sprays pesticides in a fine mist to kill mosquitoes and other outdoor insects. Nozzles around the dwelling, lawn, landscaping, house, or fence connect by tubing to an insecticide supply. Some systems operate at timed intervals.
  • Patio: The property has a patio.
  • Porch: The property has a porch.
  • Rear Porch: The property has a rear porch.
  • Screened: The property has a screened patio or porch.
  • Side Porch: The property has a side porch.
  • Terrace: The property has a terrace.
  • Wrap Around: The property has a wraparound patio or porch.
