General Field Help:

The verification or certification awarded to a new or existing residential or commercial structure. Add a separate certification entry for each award.

  • Austin Energy's Green Building: Rates new construction and major remodels in Austin Energy's service area and Central Texas using a 1–5 star scale based on mandatory requirements and points for sustainability, materials, construction efficiency, energy and water conservation, indoor environmental quality and innovation.
  • LEED For Homes: USGBC green building certification for new single-family homes, using mandatory requirements and points for sustainable sites, community connectivity, energy and water efficiency, materials, indoor environmental quality, education and innovation. Third-party raters assess Certified, Silver, Gold and Platinum levels.
  • ENERGY STAR Certified Homes: Whole-home certification based on energy-efficient construction practices beyond minimum code requirements. Energy efficiency is verified by a third-party organization; this differs from the ENERGY STAR product label.
  • Home Performance with ENERGY STAR: Whole-house solutions for high energy bills and comfort problems. Local sponsors recruit qualified contractors to assess and improve homes under program guidelines.
  • HERS Index Score: A certified RESNET home energy rater compares the home against a reference home of the same size and shape. A lower score indicates better energy efficiency.
  • Building America: A U.S. Department of Energy program focused on improving building performance toward cost-neutral, net-zero homes.
  • Certified Passive House: Super-insulated new homes meeting certification requirements for minimal or no heating and cooling needs.
  • PHIUS+: Super-insulated homes meeting certification requirements for minimal or no heating and cooling needs.
  • EnerPHit: Super-insulated existing homes remodeled to meet certification requirements for minimal or no heating and cooling needs.
  • Environments for Living: An independent national new-home program providing plan review, inspection, certification and performance testing for energy-efficient construction. Program guarantees may address energy usage and comfort; its Certified Green option includes indoor environmental quality, water conservation and appliance efficiency.
  • Home Energy Upgrade Certificate of Energy Efficiency Improvements: A certificate describing improvements made to an existing home through a whole-house energy upgrade, using BPI-2101 requirements. Certificates are provided by a local energy efficiency program sponsor.
  • Home Energy Upgrade Certificate of Energy Efficiency Performance: A certificate documenting one or more measures of home performance after an energy upgrade, such as HERS rating, Home Energy Score or projected/actual energy consumption.
  • Home Energy Score: A U.S. DOE home efficiency score on a 1–10 scale: 10 represents the most efficient homes, and 1 the least efficient. Assessment considers the building envelope and heating, cooling and hot-water systems under standard operating conditions, and is performed by a qualified assessor.
