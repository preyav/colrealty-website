General Field Help:

Features of the waterfront on which the property is located. The waterfront must be on or directly adjacent to the property, not just nearby.

  • Canal Front: The property is located on or adjacent to a canal.
  • Creek: The property is on or adjacent to a creek.
  • Dry/Seasonal: A waterless stream-like arrangement of stones and rocks that may also direct runoff after heavy rain.
  • Lake Front: The property is on or adjacent to the lake front.
  • Lake Privileges: The property includes rights to access the lake.
  • Pond: The property is on or adjacent to a pond.
  • River Front: The property is on or adjacent to the river front.
  • Stream: The property is on or adjacent to a stream.
  • Waterfront: The property is on or directly adjacent to the waterfront.
  • None: No waterfront features apply; cannot be combined with another feature.
  • See Remarks: Refer to the property remarks for additional details.
