General Field Help:

A list of features or description of the windows included in the sale/lease.

  • Aluminum Frames: The windows have aluminum frames.
  • Bay Window(s): The property has one or more bay windows.
  • Blinds: The property has window blinds.
  • Display Window(s): Windows normally used to display goods or products.
  • Drapes: The property has drapes.
  • Dual Pane Windows: The windows have two panes of glass.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Windows: The property has ENERGY STAR qualified windows.
  • Garden Window(s): The property has one or more garden windows.
  • Insulated Windows: The property has insulated windows.
  • Low Emissivity Windows: The property has low-emissivity windows.
  • Plantation Shutters: The property has plantation shutters.
  • Screens: The property has screens.
  • Shutters: The property has shutters.
  • Skylight: The property has skylights.
  • Solar Screens: The property has solar screens.
  • Storm Window(s): The property has storm windows.
  • Tinted Windows: Windows with a coating applied to block sunlight and harmful UV rays.
  • Triple Pane Windows: Windows with three panes of glass, spacers keeping uniform gaps, and airtight seals. Gas may fill the gaps to improve insulation and energy efficiency.
  • Vinyl Windows: Windows with frames made of vinyl.
  • Window Coverings: Materials covering windows to manage sunlight, improve weatherproofing, provide privacy or security, or serve decorative purposes.
  • Window Treatments: Interior decoration for windows or window frames, such as curtains, blinds, or valances.
  • Wood Frames: The property has wood-framed windows.
