General Field Help:

A list or description of the accessibility features included in the sale/lease.

  • Adaptable Bathroom Walls: Reinforced main bathroom walls, including bath or shower, permit future installation of grab bars with a shear force of 250 pounds and/or fixtures. Required for Enhanced Accessible.
  • Adaptable For Elevator: Stacked closets in a multistory house for possible future conversion to an elevator.
  • Approach with Ramp: At least one entrance has a clear, evenly paved walkway from parking or pedestrian arrival. Running slope does not exceed 1:12 (8.33%); cross slope does not exceed 1:50 (2%); level changes do not exceed 1/2 inch. Slopes over 5% require handrails. Includes a level landing, a 32-inch clear door opening, and adequate pathway/landing lighting. Required for Visitable.
  • Bedroom: A 60-inch turnaround or another approved configuration; closet doors with 32-inch clearance; accessible environmental controls; and multiple lighting fixtures. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: lower closet storage and remote lighting/environmental controls.
  • Ceiling Track: A ceiling track for a lift chair (Hoyer lift).
  • Central Living Area: Includes common area, hallways, a full or 3/4 bathroom, kitchen, at least one bedroom, environmental controls, and access to floors above the main floor if needed.
  • Closets: Closet doors have 32-inch clearance throughout the Central Living Area.
  • Common Area: A level entertaining area with 36-inch passage through and around the space. Required for Visitable.
  • Customized Wheelchair Accessible: Accessibility customized for a specific size or style of wheelchair or scooter.
  • Doors: Minimum 32-inch clear passage; lever handle; threshold no higher than 1/2 inch, beveled on both sides when over 1/4 inch. Required for Visitable or Enhanced Accessible.
  • Electronic Environmental Controls: Thermostats and security controls are on the floor with the central living area. Light switches and thermostats are 42–48 inches above the floor with side-to-side access; electrical plugs are at least 18 inches above the floor. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: rocker-style or lighted switches and automatic/remote environmental controls.
  • Elevator Installed: An elevator with a minimum 32-inch door and minimum 36 × 48-inch turning radius.
  • Enhanced Accessibility: The Central Living Area is fully accessible for lifelong living by all residents, regardless of ability. A wheelchair user or person with another disability can perform personal and housekeeping tasks, while other residents can perform basic tasks more easily. Includes all Visitable features.
  • Entrance: Minimum 32-inch-wide entrance; threshold no higher than 1/2 inch, beveled on both sides when over 1/4 inch; lever handle for egress. Required for Visitable. Optional: covered entry, bench near the door, motion-detection lights, accessible peephole or other viewing method, and a house number easily seen by emergency responders.
  • Exterior Wheelchair Lift: A mechanical wheelchair lift outside the home to facilitate a barrier-free approach.
  • For Hearing-Impairment: Wiring for flashing lights and/or a vibrating smoke alarm, doorbell, or other alerts.
  • Full Bath: A 60-inch turnaround or another approved configuration; roll-in shower or space for a shower chair or bathtub transfer bench; slip-resistant bathroom and bathing surfaces; roll-under or side-access sink with padded trap; and lighting directly over bathing areas as well as general lighting. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: removable sink cabinet doors/base; properly backed grab bars around toilet and bath with 250-pound shear force; handheld or height-adjustable shower head; automatic anti-scald water controls; controls offset toward the front edge for easier access; and a raised toilet, 17–19 inches minimum.
  • Fully Accessible: The property is fully accessible for lifelong living by all residents, regardless of ability. A wheelchair user or person with another disability can perform personal and housekeeping tasks, while other residents can perform basic tasks more easily. Includes all Visitable features for Enhanced Accessible.
  • Grip-Accessible Features: Doors, faucets, and other mechanisms throughout the central living area use levers, hands-free operation, or controls usable with a closed fist or weak hands. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: D-ring wire handles or easy-touch cabinet/drawer latches.
  • Hallway(s): Minimum 36-inch-wide hallways, preferably 42 inches, or a suitable alternative for the individual configuration. At least one lighting fixture is present.
  • Hand Rails: Rails mounted along walls to help prevent falls and aid walking or transfers.
  • Kitchen: A 40-inch clear turnaround, or 36-inch clearance with under-counter space for a T-turn, unobstructed by fixtures. Roll-under/adaptable sink with padded trap or side access; roll-out shelves in at least 50% of lower cabinets; roll-under work area with 36-inch clearance; and task lighting with adaptable controls. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: removable under-sink cabinet doors/base, accessible upper cabinets, at least 36 inches for a side-by-side refrigerator, and contrasting counter edges for visual accessibility.
  • Kitchen Appliances: Front or side stove controls at countertop height, oven side-access door at counter level, and microwave at counter level. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: raised dishwasher and clear microwave work area below or to the right.
  • Lower Fixtures: Easily accessed switches for smaller residents or wheelchair access.
  • Reinforced Floors: Floors reinforced for bariatric needs, power wheelchairs, therapeutic tubs, or heavy medical equipment.
  • Safe Emergency Egress from Home: At least two no-step accessible exits from the Central Living Area. Window locks are 19–54 inches above the floor and operable with a closed fist. Sleeping-area emergency windows require minimal effort to open. Required for Enhanced Accessible.
  • Smart Technology: Computer-controlled smart-home products, such as voice controls, voice reminders, or remote monitoring of individuals with dementia.
  • Stair Lift: A professionally installed motorized rail lift for an interior or exterior stairway.
  • Stairway: Handrails on both sides, extended where possible, with 250-pound shear force; adequate lighting; nonslip treads; and, if carpeted, secured low-pile carpet. Required for Enhanced Accessible. Optional: high-contrast stair treads for visibility.
  • Standby Generator: Backup generator for medication refrigeration, life-sustaining medical equipment, or essential room temperature control.
  • Therapeutic Whirlpool: A properly installed therapeutic whirlpool.
  • Visitable: A wheelchair user can easily enter the home and access the main Common Area, at least a half bathroom, and the hall leading to that bathroom.
  • Visitor Bathroom: The bathroom nearest the Common Area, at least a half bath. Door has at least 32-inch clear opening and lever handles. Includes a 60-inch turnaround or approved alternative, such as 30 × 48-inch clear space when the door opens outward, and a properly backed toilet-area grab bar. Required for Visitable.
  • Walker-Accessible Stairs: Treads at least 25 inches deep, with maximum 4-inch rise and minimum 36-inch width. May be a custom feature in addition to a ramp approach.
  • Washer/Dryer: Raised clothes washer and/or dryer with front controls and side-opening doors that both open toward the center.
