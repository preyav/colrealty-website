General Field Help:

Appliances and/or equipment in or around the house that may convey with the sale of the property. Only the contract signed by each party is enforceable.

  • Bar Fridge: A refrigerator sized or built to be part of a bar.
  • Built-In Electric Oven: A built-in electric oven.
  • Built-In Electric Range: A built-in electric range.
  • Built-In Freezer: A built-in freezer.
  • Built-In Gas Oven: A built-in gas oven.
  • Built-In Gas Range: A built-in gas range.
  • Built-In Oven(s): Ovens built into a special tall unit to provide a convenient working height.
  • Built-In Range: A built-in range where the fuel type is not specified.
  • Built-In Refrigerator: A built-in refrigerator.
  • Convection Oven: A fan-assisted oven that circulates air around food.
  • Cooktop: A kitchen appliance designed for cooking food using direct heat.
  • Dishwasher: A mechanical device for cleaning dishware and cutlery.
  • Disposal: An electrically powered unit beneath a kitchen sink, between the drain and trap, that shreds food waste into small pieces to pass through plumbing.
  • Down Draft: A vent in the cooktop surface that draws cooking fumes and smoke downward as an alternative to a hooded cooktop/range.
  • Dryer: A clothes dryer.
  • Electric Cooktop: A cooktop that produces heat using electricity rather than gas. Induction cooktops are electric, but not all electric cooktops use induction.
  • Electric Range: An oven and cooktop that generate heat using electricity.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Appliances: The property includes ENERGY STAR qualified appliances.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Dishwasher: The property includes an ENERGY STAR qualified dishwasher.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Dryer: The property includes an ENERGY STAR qualified clothes dryer.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Freezer: The property includes an ENERGY STAR qualified freezer.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Refrigerator: The property includes an ENERGY STAR qualified refrigerator.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Washer: The property includes an ENERGY STAR qualified clothes washer.
  • ENERGY STAR Qualified Water Heater: The property includes an ENERGY STAR qualified water heater.
  • Exhaust Fan: The cooktop has an exhaust fan.
  • Freezer: The property includes a freezer.
  • Freezer Free-Standing: The freezer is free-standing rather than built-in.
  • Gas Cooktop: A cooktop that produces heat using gas rather than electricity.
  • Gas Range: An oven and cooktop that generate heat using gas.
  • Humidifier: The property includes a humidity control device or system.
  • Ice Maker: The property includes an ice maker.
  • Indoor Grill: The property has an indoor grill.
  • Induction Cooktop: An electric cooktop based on magnetic induction rather than heating coils.
  • Instant Hot Water: The property has a circulatory or instant hot water system.
  • Microwave: The property includes a microwave.
  • Oven: The property includes an oven.
  • Oven Electric: An oven heated by electricity, typically using heating coils.
  • Oven Free-Standing Electric: An oven that is free-standing rather than built-in and uses electricity to produce heat.
  • Oven Free-Standing Gas: An oven that is free-standing rather than built-in and uses gas to produce heat.
  • Oven Gas: An oven heated by gas.
  • Oven-Double: A built-in fixture with either two ovens or one oven and one microwave, usually built into a kitchen cabinet.
  • Plumbed For Ice Maker: The property has plumbing for an ice maker.
  • Portable Dishwasher: The property includes a portable dishwasher.
  • Propane Cooktop: A gas cooktop that uses propane from a local tank or a house-wide propane system.
  • Range: A single unit containing both an oven and a cooktop.
  • Range Free-Standing Electric: A free-standing range that uses electricity to heat its oven and cooktop.
  • Range Free-Standing: A free-standing range rather than a built-in range.
  • Range Free-Standing Gas: A free-standing range that uses gas to heat its oven and cooktop.
  • Range Hood: The range has a hooded exhaust.
  • Refrigerator: The property includes a refrigerator.
  • Refrigerator Free-Standing: The refrigerator is free-standing rather than built-in.
  • Self Cleaning Oven: The oven has a self-cleaning feature.
  • Solar Hot Water: The hot water heater has a passive or active solar component.
  • Stainless Steel Appliance(s): Some or all included appliances are stainless steel.
  • Tankless Water Heater: A tankless water heater is included with the property.
  • Trash Compactor: The property has a trash compactor.
  • Vented Exhaust Fan: The cooktop has an exhaust fan that is vented.
  • Warming Drawer: The property has a warming drawer.
  • Washer: The property includes a clothes washer.
  • Washer/Dryer: The property includes a clothes washer and dryer.
  • Washer/Dryer Stacked: The property has a stacked clothes washer and dryer.
  • Water Heater-Electric: The property has an electric water heater.
  • Water Heater-Gas: The property has a gas water heater.
  • Water Purifier: The property has a water purifier.
  • Water Purifier Owned: The water purifier is owned rather than rented or leased.
  • Water Purifier Rented: The water purifier is provided under a rental or lease agreement.
  • Water Softener: The property has a water softening system.
  • Water Softener Owned: The water softening system is owned rather than rented or leased.
  • Water Softener Rented: The water softening system is provided under a rental or lease agreement.
  • Wine Cooler: The property includes a wine cooler.
  • Wine Refrigerator: The property includes a wine refrigerator.
